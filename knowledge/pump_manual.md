# Centrifugal pump service manual (excerpt): CP-series end-suction pumps

Applies to pumps P-07 and P-11 to P-14 on Line 3 (CP-80 frame, 15 kW, 2900 rpm, 6309-2Z bearings on the drive end).

## Bearing wear
**What it sounds like:** a steady grinding or rumbling that rises in pitch over days. Early wear adds energy in the high tones (above about 2 kHz) on top of the normal hum. Late wear gives a growl with a rhythmic beat.
**Why it happens:** lubricant breakdown, contamination, overload, or misalignment loading the bearing.
**What to do:** plan a bearing change at the next planned stop if the pump is still within temperature limits (housing below 80 °C). If the sound is very unusual, or the housing is hot, stop the pump within the shift. Continuing to run a failing bearing risks shaft damage and seal failure, which turns a 2-hour bearing job into a 2-day overhaul.
**Part:** drive-end bearing 6309-2Z (stores code BRG-6309). Typical repair time: 2 hours with two technicians.

## Misalignment
**What it sounds like:** a pulsing hum at running speed, sometimes a knock once per revolution; coupling area noisier than the pump body.
**Why it happens:** soft foot, thermal growth, or a disturbed base after maintenance.
**What to do:** check the coupling with a straight edge or laser tool at the next stop. Re-shim the motor feet. Not usually urgent unless vibration is rising.
**Part:** coupling insert (stores code CPL-80).

## Cavitation
**What it sounds like:** gravel or marbles rattling inside the casing, irregular crackling, louder at high flow.
**Why it happens:** low suction pressure: blocked strainer, low tank level, or a throttled suction valve.
**What to do:** check the suction strainer and tank level first, before stopping the pump. Prolonged cavitation erodes the impeller and damages the mechanical seal.
**Part:** mechanical seal (stores code SEAL-80) if leaking after cavitation.

## Loose impeller
**What it sounds like:** metallic knocking that changes with flow.
**What to do:** stop at the next opportunity; inspect the impeller nut and key.
