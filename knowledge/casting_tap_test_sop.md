# SOP-QA-017: Acoustic tap test for castings

**Purpose:** screen castings for cracks and gross porosity before machining.

1. Place the casting on the rubber pad so it can ring freely. Do not hold it by hand while tapping.
2. Tap once with the brass hammer at the marked tap point, with the same force each time.
3. A sound casting rings clearly for a moment after the tap. A cracked or porous casting sounds dull and the ring dies away quickly.
4. Tap each part once. If a part sounds dull, tap it once more to double-check.
5. Parts confirmed dull are set aside on the red rack and the batch is put on hold until quality engineering decides. Record the part number on the batch card.
6. If two or more parts in a batch are dull, quarantine the whole batch and inform the quality engineer.
