# SOP-SAF-004: Lockout / tagout (LOTO) for rotating equipment

Before any hands-on work on a pump, motor, conveyor, compressor or coach equipment:

1. **Tell people.** Inform the shift supervisor and the operators on the line that the machine will stop. Announce it on the floor.
2. **Stop the machine** using its normal stop control. Do not use the emergency stop for a planned stop.
3. **Isolate energy.** Open the isolator at the motor control centre. For pumps, also close the suction and discharge valves.
4. **Lock and tag.** Each person working fits their own padlock and a signed tag to the isolator. One lock per person, never shared.
5. **Release stored energy.** Bleed pressure from lines, block gravity loads, let rotating parts come to rest.
6. **Try to start.** Press start at the local control to confirm the machine does not run, then return the control to off.
7. **Work.**
8. **Remove locks** only when the work is finished, guards are back, and everyone is clear. The last person to remove a lock tells the supervisor.

A machine that is locked out is shown as "out of service" in the maintenance system until the work order is closed.
