# Coach wheel examination: hammer (ring) test

**Applies to:** coach wheel sets during the examination on the pit line.

1. With the coach stationary and protected (red flag or lamp on both ends, line blocked), the examiner taps each wheel with the test hammer near the rim.
2. A sound wheel gives a clear, ringing note. A cracked wheel, or a loose tyre on a tyred wheel, gives a dull, flat note.
3. A wheel that sounds dull must be tapped again and inspected visually for cracks at the rim, plate and hub.
4. If a wheel is confirmed dull or cracked, **mark the coach sick**: it must not run. Inform train control and the depot supervisor, and raise a repair memo for wheel set replacement.
5. Record the coach number, wheel position and result in the examination register.

**Wheel positions** are numbered 1 to 8 from the coupler end, left side first.
